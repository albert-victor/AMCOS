Kuhusu SisiAbout Us

MGOWELO AMCOS — Ushirika wa Kilimo na Masoko MGOWELO AMCOS — Agricultural Marketing Cooperative

MGOWELO AMCOS ni ushirika wa wakulima wa Mgowelo, Iringa, unaowahudumia wanachama kupitia usimamizi wa masoko ya mazao, huduma za kifedha za ushirika, na uratibu wa taarifa za wanachama kwa uwazi. MGOWELO AMCOS is a farmer cooperative based in Mgowelo, Iringa, serving members through crop marketing coordination, cooperative financial services, and transparent member records management.

Mfumo huu wa kidijitali unarahisisha usajili, malipo, hisa, mikutano ya uongozi, na mawasiliano ya taarifa ndani ya ushirika. This digital platform simplifies registration, payments, shares, governance meetings, and internal communication across the cooperative.

Maswali ya Kawaida Common Questions

Nani anaweza kutumia mfumo huu? Who can use this system?

Mfumo huu ni kwa wanachama, viongozi, wafanyakazi na wasimamizi wa MGOWELO AMCOS. Kila mtumiaji ana akaunti na jukumu lake (mfano PA-RC, mhasibu, karani). This system is for members, leaders, staff and administrators of MGOWELO AMCOS. Each user has an account and role (e.g. PA-RC, accountant, secretary).

Ninawezaje kuingia kwa mara ya kwanza? How do I sign in for the first time?

Bofya Ingia juu ya ukurasa, ingiza namba yako ya simu na nenosiri ulilopewa na ofisi. Ikiwa huna akaunti, wasiliana na karani wa ushirika wako. Click Login at the top, enter your phone number and password provided by the office. If you have no account, contact your cooperative secretary.

Je, ninaweza kuona akiba na mikopo yangu mtandaoni? Can I view my savings and loans online?

Ndiyo. Baada ya kuingia, wanachama wanaweza kuona salio la akiba, historia ya malipo, hali ya mikopo na ripoti zingine kwa uwazi. Yes. After signing in, members can view savings balance, payment history, loan status and other reports transparently.

Mfumo unasaidia malipo ya kidijitali? Does the system support digital payments?

Ndiyo. Mfumo unasaidia kurekodi na kufuatilia malipo kupitia M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, benki na fedha taslimu — kwa uwazi wa kifedha. Yes. The system records and tracks payments via M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, bank and cash — for financial transparency.

Ninapata msaada wapi ikiwa nimesahau nenosiri? Where do I get help if I forgot my password?

Tumia kiungo cha Weka Upya Nenosiri kwenye ukurasa wa kuingia, au piga simu ofisi yetu: +255 754 800 123. Use the Reset Password link on the login page, or call our office: +255 754 800 123.

Je, data yangu salama? Is my data secure?

Ndiyo. Mfumo unatumia uthibitishaji salama, kumbukumbu za ukaguzi (audit trails) na udhibiti wa majukumu ili kulinda taarifa za ushirika na wanachama. Yes. The system uses secure authentication, audit trails and role-based access to protect cooperative and member information.
