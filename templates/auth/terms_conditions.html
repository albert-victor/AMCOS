Kiswahili

CHAMA CHA USHIRIKA CHA WAKULIMA WA MAZAO MCHANGANYIKO MGOWELO — Wilaya ya Kilolo, Mkoa wa Iringa, Tanzania. S.L.P …. KILOLO.

1. Utangulizi

Chama kinakuza uchumi na maisha ya wanachama kupitia pembejeo, mbegu bora, masoko ya mazao mchanganyiko (tumbaku, pamba, alizeti, ufuta, karanga, soya, matunda na mboga), kilimo cha block, elimu, na miradi inayoidhinishwa na Mkutano Mkuu.

2. Sifa za uanachama

  • Umri wa chini wa kuwa mwanachama: miaka 15; wa uongozi: miaka 18.
  • Lazima uishi katika eneo la shughuli za chama na ukubali masharti ya chama.
  • Ushiriki katika kilimo cha kisasa au ufugaji na kuendeleza malengo ya chama.
  • Akili timamu na utayari wa kuchangia mtaji na kutunza mali ya chama kwa uaminifu.
  • Asiwe na shughuli binafsi zinazofananishwa na za chama.

3. Maombi na kukubaliwa

  • Andika barua na ujaze fomu rasmi uwasilishe kwa Mwenyekiti.
  • Bodi inajadili na kutoa pendekezo la kukubali au kukataa.
  • Mkutano Mkuu una uamuzi wa mwisho wa kukubali au kukataa.
  • Baada ya kukubaliwa, lipa kiingilio na nunua hisa kama ilivyo kwenye katiba.
  • Kila mwanachama apewe kitambulisho; fomu za waliokataliwa na ada zinashughulikiwa kwa mujibu wa katiba.

4. Kiingilio na hisa

  • Kiingilio: TZS 100,000 (haitarejeshwa ukikoma uanachama).
  • Hisa: Mwanachama alazimike kuwa na angalau hisa 10 ndani ya miaka 2 (TZS 100,000 kwa kila hisa). Kwa kuanzia, hisa 5 (jumla TZS 500,000).
  • Ardhi kama hisa: Kiwango cha chini ni ekari 10; unaweza kuchangia zaidi. Ardhi inamilikiwa na kusimamiwa na chama; mapato yanagawanywa kwa ekari baada ya gharama.
  • Aliyekamilisha kulipa hisa zote atapewa hati ya kumiliki hisa.

5. Haki na wajibu

  • Kushiriki kikamilifu katika mikutano na shughuli za chama.
  • Mtu mmoja kura moja bila kujali idadi ya hisa.
  • Haki ya kuchagua na kuchaguliwa; haki sawa juu ya mali ya chama.
  • Wajibu wa kufuata sheria za ushirika, masharti ya chama, na maamuzi ya Mkutano Mkuu.

6. Kukoma uanachama

Uanachama unaweza kukoma kwa kifo, kuhamia, kutoshiriki, kukiuka kanuni, kichaa kilichothibitishwa, kuacha kuwa na hisa, kujiuzulu (taarifa ya miezi 3), na sababu nyingine kwenye katiba na Sheria ya Vyama vya Ushirika.

7. Bodi ya Uongozi

Mkutano Mkuu huchagua Bodi yenye wajumbe wasiopungua 5 na wasiozidi 9, wanaochagua Mwenyekiti, Makamu, Katibu, Makamu Katibu, na Mhazini. Viongozi hutumikia miaka 5 na kulinda katiba ya chama.

8. Usajili wako kwenye mfumo huu

Kwa kujisajili mtandaoni unathibitisha taarifa zako ni sahihi, unastahili uanachama, unakubali kulipa kiingilio, na unakubali kukaguliwa na Bodi kabla ya kukamilisha uanachama. Baada ya idhini ya Bodi utahitajika kununua hisa kufungua huduma kamili za mwanachama.

English

MGOWELO MULTI-FARMERS ASSOCIATION LTD — Kilolo, Iringa Region, Tanzania. P.O. Box …, Kilolo.

1. Introduction

The cooperative promotes members' economic and social welfare through access to inputs, quality seeds, markets for mixed crops (tobacco, cotton, sunflower, sesame, groundnuts, soya, fruits and vegetables), block farming, education, and joint projects approved by the General Meeting.

2. Membership eligibility

  • Minimum age to join: 15 years; minimum age for leadership: 18 years.
  • Must reside in the cooperative's area of operation and accept society rules.
  • Must engage in modern farming or livestock and support the cooperative's objectives.
  • Must be of sound mind and willing to contribute capital and act faithfully when entrusted with society duties and assets.
  • Must not run personal activities that conflict with the society's business.

3. Application and approval

  • Apply in writing using the official form submitted to the Chairperson.
  • The Board discusses and recommends acceptance or rejection.
  • The General Meeting has the final decision on membership.
  • After acceptance, pay the entry fee and purchase shares as defined in this constitution.
  • Each member receives a membership ID; rejected applicants' forms and fees are handled per constitution (entry fee is not refunded after acceptance).

4. Entry fee and shares

  • Entry fee: TZS 100,000 (non-refundable if membership ends).
  • Shares: A member must hold at least ten (10) shares within two years (TZS 100,000 per share). Initially, five (5) shares may be paid (TZS 500,000 total).
  • Land as shares: Minimum contribution is 10 hectares; members may contribute more. Land contributed is owned and managed by the society; income is shared by hectares after costs.
  • Upon full payment of shares, a share ownership certificate is issued.

5. Rights and duties

  • Participate fully in meetings and cooperative activities.
  • One member, one vote regardless of number of shares.
  • Right to elect and be elected; equal rights over society property.
  • Duty to follow cooperative law, bylaws, and General Meeting decisions; pay contributions and debts owed to the society.

6. End of membership

Membership may end due to death, relocation, non-participation, breach of cooperative regulations, proven mental incapacity, loss of required shares, resignation (with 3 months' written notice), or other causes in the constitution and Cooperative Societies Act.

7. Board of Directors

The General Meeting elects a Board of not fewer than five (5) and not more than nine (9) members, who elect Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, Secretary, Vice Secretary, and Treasurer. Leaders serve five-year terms and must protect the constitution.

8. Your registration in this system

By registering online you confirm that your details are true, you meet eligibility criteria, you agree to pay the entry fee, and you accept review by the Board before full membership is completed. After board approval you must purchase shares to access full member services.
